Privacy Policy

For AlexSanr AI Team · Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AlexSanr AI Team (“the App”) handles information when an authorized user connects a YouTube account and uses the App’s YouTube analytics features.

1. Operator and contact

The App is an independently operated tool for managing and analyzing the operator’s YouTube channel. Privacy questions: stopgenocidinworld@gmail.com. Replace this placeholder with a monitored email address before publishing.

2. Information accessed

With permission, the App may access read-only YouTube information through official Google APIs, including:

  • Channel and video metadata, such as identifiers, titles, publication dates and duration;
  • YouTube Analytics metrics made available by Google, such as views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources and audience summaries;
  • OAuth authorization information needed to maintain the connection.

The configured authorization does not request permission to upload, edit, delete or manage YouTube videos, and does not request monetization or revenue data.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide channel and video analytics, compare performance, prepare reports, and support analysis and recommendations inside AlexSanr AI Team. The App does not sell YouTube API data or use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Information may be processed and stored locally on the computer or server where the App is installed, including in its application database. OAuth credentials are stored separately from ordinary analytics data. On supported Windows installations, the OAuth token is protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), which binds protection to the Windows user and device context.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. The operator should restrict access to the computer, application directory, backups and configuration files.

5. Sharing

The App does not sell or rent personal information. Data is sent to Google as needed to authenticate and retrieve information through Google APIs. The operator may choose to include limited analytics summaries in reports; the operator is responsible for where those reports are shared.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored analytics information may be retained while the feature is in use. Disconnecting the Google account revokes authorization where supported and removes the locally stored OAuth token. Previously collected analytics records may remain and may need to be deleted separately from the App’s local data directory or database.

7. Revoking Google access

You can review or revoke access at Google Account — Third-party connections.

8. Google API Services

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google services are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is an administrative analytics tool and is not intended for children under 13. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the App’s functionality or data practices change. Contact: stopgenocidinworld@gmail.com.
