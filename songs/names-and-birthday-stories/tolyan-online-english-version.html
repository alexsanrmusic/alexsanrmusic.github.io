Yo…
Thirteen years - not a little kid anymore, my bro,
But honestly… still loves Minecraft, you know.
Anatoly online… status: “do not disturb”,
The party starts when he finally goes outside for sure.

Tolik knows Windows better than the school tech class,
If something crashes - everybody calls him fast.
With headphones on, Talyan is out of reach,
Some raid, some match, some new world to breach.
Store mission - level “hardcore” run,
“Go buy some bread” - feels like esports fun.
But he’s a kind guy, no flex, no shield, no show,
He can give it all away… just like that, you know.
His English is sharp, mom’s like: “What is this?”
He calmly answers: “Mom, relax, please.”
And while the whole class tries not to mess it up,
Tosik Googles how to set a server up.

And even if school feels like a boss fight,
Talyan’s got his own strange vibe in life.
A little in the clouds, but fire in his soul,
And when he laughs, the whole house feels whole.
Hey Anatoly, don’t rush to grow,
The world can wait — let it take it slow.
Play, joke, dream, just fly away,
But still… sometimes go outside and play.
