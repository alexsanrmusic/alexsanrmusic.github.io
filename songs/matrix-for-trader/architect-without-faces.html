They don’t wear uniforms,
they don’t wave flags,
but they decide
where the storm begins
and where the world exhales.

They live in shadows under polished logos,
Think tanks whisper - RAND, CSIS, cold and focused.
No armor on, just suits in muted grey,
Counting wars like margins at the end of the day.
No gunfire - just a quiet “Send”,
And somewhere far away the fire starts again.
One signs papers in a glass-lit room,
Another loses home before the afternoon.
They draft scenarios on boring white slides,
Maps where a thin red line decides who survives.
Down here we fight for something called truth,
Up there they price it - by the unit, per use.

The architects build without faces,
They don’t need flags - they believe in numbers.
They whisper to fate like programmers of sleep:
“Load the chaos… let the country run.”

Crisis as pattern, panic as gear.
Pandemics, wars, currencies, oil,
Every line obeyed, no human recoil.
When the system cracks, no noise, no sound,
They hit “Rebuild” and spin it back around.
Faces may change, but the code stays tight,
No revolution edits that script overnight.
Deep state murmurs, lobbyist hands,
Geopolitics - a garage-band plan.
They don’t need thrones, they don’t need crowns,
Just keys to the rooms where the future’s drawn.

A graph of layers, not noise on noise.
Two-thousand-eight: launch.
Two-thousand-fourteen: shock.
Twenty-twenty: virus.
Twenty-twenty-two: the lock.
They don’t hide - they’re just unseen,
Their truths live quietly in spreadsheets clean.
I don’t run, I don’t freeze,

I don’t fight - I decode,
Where there’s chaos, I find a road.
If they build fear from days and nights,

They sketch reality in pencil lines,
Then erase borrowed dreams with a sharper design.
But I hold the pen - this page is mine,

The architects build without faces,

Architects, I read your code.
It works - but still, I stand and breathe.
You manufacture fear,
I choose to love.
