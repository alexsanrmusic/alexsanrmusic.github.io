No enemy…
No walls…
There is you…
And inside - we all…

Meanings fall like quiet rain,
Hear each sound - don’t rush the gain.
No such thing as “foe” or “kin”,
One whole world in a stream of skin.

Extremes dissolve into smoke,
Truth moves where the edges broke.
Cold flame whispers, soft and slow:
No division - only the call below.

Zeroing - a clean new move,
No guilt to carry, nothing to prove.
No enemies, no walls, no “us”,
Only rhythm pulling through the dust.
Zeroing - a newborn beat,

The world’s not split, not torn in two,

Let duality play its role,
But in its zero sleeps our soul.

Silence isn’t end, it’s start,
The world breathes deep in the hidden chart.

Zeroing - in the pulse of veins,

No below, no above, no frames,

I split it once: good and pain,
“Us” and “them” in separate lanes.
Now I see - it’s always one,

Light and shadow aren’t enemies - just steps,
Every tone in a zigzag depth.
Don’t divide into dark and bright,
Everything flows - that’s the insight.
Silence brings the sound back home,
The circle closes - moves alone.
Zeroing isn’t rest or sleep,
It’s an endless rhythm the soul must keep.

I am not alone.
I am not the other.
I am all.
I am still.
