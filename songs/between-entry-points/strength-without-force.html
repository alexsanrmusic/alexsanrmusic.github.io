Strength Without Force

♪ Слухати
Текст пісні

I stood on the edge - no faith, no strength,
Waiting for a sign, then I saw: I am the impulse, the length.
No miracle, no luck - just choice and pain,
The current won’t save you, self-control must remain.
My father’s words breathed into the night,
“Don’t fear the bottom - there’s a path beyond the height.”
I stood still, didn’t beg, didn’t run from the test,
Sat face to face with pain - teacher to chest.

Quiet, with feeling
Strength without force - I live even when weak,
Every breath holds light - the next peak I reach.
Not a hero, not a monk - just finally real,
I don’t run from the dark - I learned how to feel.

I found my ground not in walls, but in kids,
In my daughter’s laugh, in breath, in how life lives.
My wife - my compass, my son - my light,
They taught me in silence there’s no final height.
I lived by stars, never clocks on the wall,
Fell into nothing - still rose on my call.
Astrology ain’t fiction, it’s a language of roads,
Painful transits carve truth into codes.
Saturn closed doors so I’d learn how to see,
Neptune dissolved fear inside of me.
Uranus sparked fire - and I finally knew,
The point isn’t success, it’s the truth you live through.

Quiet
Strength without force - not war, but light,
Every scar becomes sacred when faced upright.
Don’t chase victory - it lives in breath,
Live without proving, beyond fear of death.

Years outside the system, no race, no crown,
But heat inside - not fire, a forged compound.
I’m no hermit, I just became whole,
Found my body in a world with no role.
Morning tea - my ritual ground,
Silence - a law, not a vow, not a sound.
I don’t gamble, I question fate,
Every trade asks me straight:
“Are you clean with the world? Do you breathe what’s alive?”
If the answer is yes - you already survive.

I wrote signs not for mystic display,
But to feel my rhythm - physics at play.
A whisper said “listen,”
Fire said “release,”
The path said “walk,”
Just don’t fall beneath.
Breath is my temple, silence my guide,
God’s name within me - I witness the tide.

I don’t search for peace - I wear it in style,
Not force that moves me, but meaning and fire.
Ten years outside the world - yet never alone,
My path lives in me. I’m not a shadow - I’m flame in the stone.
