Afterword. Gratitude

🇬🇧 АнглійськаBetween entry points: Testament of the living2026
♪ Слухати
Текст пісні

Not the ending, not the score,
Just the road and one step more.
Just a voice that stayed awake,
When inside I felt erased.

This is the voice of a man who survived the rain,
Walked through fear and pain, but stayed sane.
I wasn’t chasing trophies, I was chasing my name,
And I found who I am - through love, not fame.
Thanks to everyone who stood nearby,
When the storm washed roads and skies went dry.
Those who didn’t leave when the night got long,
Silent, but present - like a window strong.

Without you I wouldn’t be who I am,
Without you I wouldn’t understand.
Every word, every look you gave
Was invisible light - the strength that stayed.
...Every word...
...the strength that stayed...

Thank you for faith when I fell apart,
When I searched for myself in the dark.
Those who just stayed, no need to explain,
Who gave no advice - just love, no chains.
To family, to friends, to the quiet ones too,
Who stayed when the world just froze and withdrew.
You didn’t leave, you waited, you called,
You are my home, my open hall.

Without you I wouldn’t be who I am,
Without you I wouldn’t know this man.
And if life is a game we play till the end,
You are the meaning, the spark, the flame.
...And if life...
...the spark, the flame...

I’m not alone, I’m part of you all,
Your light guides me when shadows fall.
As long as I breathe - I give thanks, I do,
Every breath a blessing, passing it through.

This book is not the end of the line,
I just want to keep walking, page by page, in time.
As long as I breathe - I’m writing my truth.
Thank you. Everyone.
I’m still alive.
O-o-o-o-o...I’m still alive...
O-o-o-o-o...I’m still alive...
...alive..
