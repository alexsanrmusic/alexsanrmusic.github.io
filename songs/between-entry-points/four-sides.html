Four Sides

♪ Слухати
Текст пісні

I mapped a chart, not of the world - of me,
Not healing the body - seeking the fire’s key.
Not chasing diagnosis, but the root of pain,
Where weakness births my sunrise again.

Four sides - my act of control,
Where body, breath, rhythm, and will patrol.
Body as temple, I mend the base,
Taste - not habit, but understanding’s grace.
Each meal - a test with meaning,
Does it warm, heal, or trap my leaning?
I threw out the poison, cleared the block,
Ate not just food - but what could unlock.
Where heat - cooled, where weakness - to light,
East studied - seeking answers right.

I became a hero without stage or prize,
No war, no blood, yet crossed a hundred skies.
Each day mine - a fight for me,
I chose to live, not wait for decree.
I built a temple from simple bricks,
From pain, from fear, from countless nights’ tricks.
Now I stand, not praying, but breathing through,
I’m alive - my body still carrying true.

Breath - my password, my access to self,
Not chaos, but rhythm along narrow shelf.
Inhale - a prayer, exhale - the law,
Calm - not a gift, but my marathon’s draw.
Breathing in square - four walls around,
Every breath — an anchor, silence profound.
Rhythm - my friend, my day aligned,
No rush, just pattern, purpose defined.
Morning ritual, meals as support,
Sleep - returning to self, no report.
Chaos broke, I built my own weave,
Rhythm healed where judgment would leave.
Inherited will, mine not borrowed,
Not “victim of sickness,” but strength I harbored.
Every morning - a fresh blank page,
Life - no diagnosis, I’m the sage.

I became a hero without stage or prize,
No war, yet crossed a hundred skies.
Each day - my fight for me,
I chose to live, not wait for decree.
I built a temple from simple bricks,
From pain, from fear, from countless nights’ tricks.
Now I stand, not praying, but breathing through,
I’m alive - my body still carrying true.

Son nearby - breath, my quiet beacon,
When I couldn’t rise - he held it even.
Without words he said: “Live - don’t give in,”
And I stood up - into my new win.
Daughter grown - brighter than dawn,
In her eyes my answer and bond.
Wife - my frame, my fire, my support,
She wiped the pain without extra report.
Brother - a shoulder, words not required,
He stood when I fell, dreams expired.
Friends - not from chat, not likes on screen,
But those who held fog in silence unseen.
We drank tea, a vow to fate:
“Live while you can. Breathe - your state.”
I stood by the window, for the first time truly,
Felt my heart - no fear, no tears unruly.

Trading - not money, but a map to way,
In candlesticks I learned to save my day.
Stop - not failure, but lesson form,
Life - a trade, yet outside any norm.
Lost - didn’t fall, just understood the plot,
The world’s not enemy - just sharp and hot.

Now my regime - not a cage, but light,
Where every dawn - my personal insight.
Not rich, but whole, half a century passed,
Being husband. Being father. Being steadfast.
