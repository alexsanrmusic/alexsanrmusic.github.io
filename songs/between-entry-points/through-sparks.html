Through the sparks of matches, smoke, and ruins,
Everything was rough, but we kept getting up.

Childhood smelled of glue, fire, and paper,
Matches, slingshots, alleys, daring games.
Climbing gardens where walnuts and plums grew,
The world was simple - no guilt, no schemes.
A friend beside me - like a bolt in the engine,
Life pulled apart, but kept us in the pattern.
I swam, I healed, became a brother’s keeper,
The world crumbled, but I stood within it.
Books, yards, camps, fields,
Childhood burned bright like a vivid candle.

Through pain and fear - I walked, never giving in,
Through darkness and shadow - I found myself within.
Don’t seek excuses, build your own way,
From rubble - a building, from ashes - the core.

The USSR cracked like an old gramophone,
Chernobyl, coupons, street law unknown.
Underworld romance, the street was the school,
Your word was power, your fist a tool.
Shoemaker at seventeen, glue and rubber,
Every order - a chance, every winter - a mine.
Switched firms, built houses,
Calluses on hands, yet life kept moving.
Offices, loans, debts, gambling machines,
Jackpot - a moment, then empty routines.
Talent faded like spring rain,
But I remained - not in dirt, but in the flame.

Through pain and fear - I walked, never giving in,
Through darkness and shadow - I found myself within.
Don’t seek excuses, build your own way,

Illness, loss - mom, dad,
Life bent me like a pencil with no heart.
Five years in fog - antidepressants,
Panic screams, restless nights, constant variants.
But I climbed out, survived, broke the cycle,
Pain became a friend, not an enemy.
Stomach, anxiety, sleepless nights,
Through pain and darkness - I looked them in the eyes.
Exchange, charts, life by candles,
Mistakes hit like beats across my shoulders.
Wife - my anchor, never left the storm,
Kids - my purpose, my new home.

Through pain and fear - I walked, never giving in,
Through darkness and shadow - I found myself within.
Don’t seek excuses, build your own way,
From rubble - a building, from ashes - the core.

And if tomorrow strikes again,
Know - nothing was wasted, all was meant.
Through sparks - the path, through pain - the calm,
You’re alive, brother, and that’s the fight.
Not for medals, not for likes,
But to stay true inside, to keep the fire alive.
You passed through storm, war, and smoke -
And became not just strong… but alive.
