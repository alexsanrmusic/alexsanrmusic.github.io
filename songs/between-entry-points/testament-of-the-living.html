I walked through life where storms meet calm,
Fell more than once - still stayed who I am.
Through pain and fear, through nights that screamed,
I found the living in simple things.

I’ve been a son, a father, a student in line,
Walked barefoot first, then learned how to run with time.
Hit zero hard, then stood up again,
Not to take from the world - but to understand.
I’m not a saint, but I know when to pause,
When silence saves more than burning applause.
I’m not rich, but I respect every bill,
Earned with clean hands, steady will.

I’m alive - and that’s the meaning I keep,
Didn’t win the game, but escaped the sheet.
I breathe, I hear, I stand, I stay,
Alive - that’s my honor every day.
I’m alive - and that’s my power, my frame,
Didn’t conquer the world, but forgave the pain.
Let my scars be a different light,
I’m alive - and that’s my right.

I didn’t beat the market - I mastered my mind,
Didn’t break myself - I rebuilt, aligned.
Every loss became a candle lit,
Every drawdown showed the road back to it.
I learned the market’s not a god or foe,
It’s a mirror that shows what you don’t want to know.
Not charts and lines, but fear inside,
Teaching you to live beyond the numbers’ side.

I’m alive - not a gambler, not a king,
Just a man with a soul, walking his ring.
I don’t chase entries, don’t wait for escape,
My path is my code, my chosen shape.
I’m alive - and that’s my joy, my space,
Not fear, not pain - just presence and grace.
The world is wide, and I exist,
Alive - and that’s the gift.

Every stop-loss draws a human line,
Where discipline beats desire every time.
Don’t chase the chance - first find your calm,
And the market itself will soften its palm.
I’m not a shadow glued to a screen,
I’m a man who learned what space can mean.
My system’s not tricks or holy codes,
It’s a path where the main thing is not to fold.
