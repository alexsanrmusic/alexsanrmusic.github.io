Acceptance. Pain as a Guide

🇬🇧 АнглійськаBetween entry points: Testament of the living2026
♪ Слухати
Текст пісні

The world whispers: “Breathe, take your time,”
I gather myself again, piece by piece, mind by mind.
Pain - not a whip, just a boundary,
Life - not a judge, but an old teacher gently.

Every morning feels like being born anew,
I breathe slow, so I won’t misstep through.
The body didn’t betray - it simply tired,
Said: “Enough, what’s needed is acquired.”
I used to run - now I read the signs,
Where pain - not an enemy, but a guide that aligns.
It tells me where I overreached,
Where I rushed, where my own lies breached.
All that hurts - a call: “Pause, feel the earth,
Stand grounded, remember your worth.”
And suddenly I see - strength is not in force,
But in listening closely to your own course.

Breathe, it will pass, clarity will shine
Through pain I returned from shadow to light.
You’re not a machine, you’re in your living right.
You’re not a machine, you’re in your living right.

Inhale - gratitude, exhale - peace,
In pause - quiet, like ether’s release.
I don’t ask to return, nor chase a miracle,
I have enough just to be - to feel full.
God’s not in temples, books, or fear,
He’s in a child’s laughter, in morning air.
In tea with my son, in my wife’s gaze,
In a chart unfolding without blame in its ways.
Pain - a teacher, life - a trainer,
Every scar - my answer to the pressure.
I don’t ask for ease or for things to be light,
I just live - and that feels right.

They told me: “You must, you should, you serve.”
I walked the sidewalk - suddenly I observed.
Not a document, not a signature, not a plan,
I’m human, not a number, not a span.
I feel the earth, and there’s my home,
No crests, no forms, no borders - just roam.
I am not a report, not an object, not a file,
I am breath - eternal, beyond any trial.

I watch the people - some asleep, some breathe,
Some trust in papers, some just perceive.
I am not loud, not daring, not saint,
I am simply alive - and in that, I paint.
