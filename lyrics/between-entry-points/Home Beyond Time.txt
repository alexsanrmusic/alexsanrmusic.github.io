Home isn’t walls, it’s living inside,
But without the others - it can’t breathe, can’t survive.
What I once lost, I finally found,
In simple routines where my roots hit the ground.

I’ve been where noise and bright candles burn,
Charts like fire, lessons hard to learn.
But life didn’t call me higher or fast,
It pulled me inward, away from the past.
With my wife - just words, no heavy demands,
Kids are time with no need to explain plans.
I don’t chase success, I don’t hunt for fame,
I’m learning to be present - and that’s my gain.

Home beyond time — my start and my ground,
Where my heart still breathes, where I’m safe and sound.
No calendar rules me, no numbers alone,
Just the smell of bread and a quiet home.

Every day’s not fear - it’s steady motion,
In daily cares I find devotion.
Kettle starts singing - that’s my sign,
Life’s still moving, rhythm in time.
Soup on the stove means the day’s alive,
The house isn’t silent - it hums, it thrives.
My tempo now isn’t bets or noise,
It’s warm calm thoughts and unforced joys.

I’m not running, I’m standing still,
And this place right here - that’s my will.
Home’s my trend, my stable chart,
Here I’m alive - not just a part.

Home beyond time - not walls, not plans,
It’s daily life where I’m myself again.
Where order lives in the smallest details,
That’s where the soul breathes easy, no fear, no fails.

Home is my altar, simple and true,
Every new day feels honest and new.
I’m not a saint, but I don’t run anymore,
My road is home - and I live in it, sure.
I live in it, sure...
I live in it, sure...
