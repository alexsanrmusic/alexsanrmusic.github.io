The market…
At first - just noise of moving candles,
But behind it — the breath of the universe.

I didn’t come here running from the pain,
Just wanted clarity inside my brain.
Turned on the screen in the early light,
And felt something shift - I’m alive.
Candles breathe like a steady heart,
Up and down — no lies in the chart.
Price whispers softly: “Look within,”
I knew no terms, no rules back then.
No gaps, no leverage, no fancy names,
But I felt the current inside the frame.

The spirit of the market lives inside my breath,
Slow and steady, deeper than the stress.
I don’t chase the wave - I ride the flow,
When I stay aligned, the path just shows.
No fear in the numbers, no rush to decide,
I move with the rhythm, calm inside.
The market’s a mirror, not a test or fight,
When I’m true to myself - the signal’s right.

At first I hunted - rushed every move,
Tried to outsmart what I couldn’t prove.
Entered with fire, exited in smoke,
Learned the hard way - the market spoke.
It stripped me down, removed the noise,
Left me alone with my inner voice.
I cleared the screen, erased the clutter,
Stopped fighting waves, learned how to hover.
I synced my breathing with price and time,
Not to predict - but to align.
When I’m calm - the chart stays clear,
When I’m in fear - it turns severe.
That’s when I knew, without attack:
The market reflects my inner state back.

The spirit of the market lives inside my breath,
Clear in the silence, honest and depth.
Every loss just sharpens the soul,
Every pause brings me back to whole.
I don’t force the moment, I don’t pretend,
I listen to time, I wait till the bend.
The market’s a mirror, not a test or fight,
When I’m true to myself - the signal’s right.

It told me once, real calm and slow:
“I never move - you move, you know.
You lose when you try to grab and take,
You gain when you breathe, release, and wait.”

Now I trade not chasing the prize,
But to stay balanced, clear inside.
Every morning - not a deal, a meeting,
With the part of me that stays unshaken.
Drawdown’s not pain - it’s vision refined,
The chart’s a prayer moving through time.
When price breaks high, no rush, no scream,
I smile - something grew in me.

I breathe with the market, slow and alive,
Every small moment reminds me I’m fine.
I don’t need to force it, I don’t need control,
When I’m in the flow, I’m already whole.
Let the candles move like waves in the night,
I trust my balance, I trust my sight.
The market don’t judge, it just reflects,
Who you are inside - that’s what you get.

Not for fame, not for the score,
But for presence, the inner core.
I don’t rush - I move in awareness,
The market is my path.
My breath.
