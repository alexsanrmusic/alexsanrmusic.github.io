Every scar is my teacher,
Every fear - a measure of my way.

I sank without a trace, no hand, no shore,
Quietly carried half of Babylon on my core.
Demons traced my steps, with no intent,
Only in me did they find icons meant.
I never sought excuses in fate,
Never cursed the sky - it lives in me, innate.
Pain - not a bullet, pain - a sign,
Every hit a ticket out of the cage, by design.

I walked on glass, but reached the light,
Fell a hundred times - rose without fright.
The path’s not straight - it’s alive like a scar,
My heart burns - I remember every spar.
Darkness isn’t my foe - it sharpens the sight,
Light doesn’t come without pain, that’s right.
Life by the inch - my battle plan,
All I am - forged there, where I stand.

I didn’t pray, I just breathed,
Listened as fear transformed to heat.
Saturn tested - gave no discounts,
Mars whispered: “Keep the rhythm, mount.”
Mercury twisted roads and signs,
I untangled fears like blooming vines.
Jupiter gave me not fame, but sight,
Even in pain - harmony ignites.

Pain isn’t punishment, pain - a guide,
I fell - I rose, like a rebel inside.
I hear in the silence my pulse, my light,
Everything’s already here - just fear’s out of sight.

I walked on glass, but reached the light,
Fell a hundred times - rose without fright.
My path is uneven, but mine to the end,
Pain turned me into a creator, my friend.

Shadows are gone, but left a trace,
I became myself, though roads erase.
Life by the inch, step by step,
Now I live - not by fear, but blessed.
