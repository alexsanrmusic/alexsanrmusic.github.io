Once you were everything…
Then the moment came - you split away…
You stayed alone…
But one day…
You return…

Through fear, through scars, through borrowed morals.
But in every step there was a turn,
Not a hero - just a living form.
Not control, not the peak of the way,
Just a rhythm that teaches to stay.
Not an answer, not victory’s cry -
Only silence.
Only now.

The dream brings me back to the place of light,
Where there’s no beginning, no end, no fight.
Where time whispers inside the line:
“You were everywhere -
but you lived inside.”

No labels, no borders, no names.

Just a silence that speaks its own way.

Not a struggle, not someone else’s drive -
Just the flow
that is always alive.
