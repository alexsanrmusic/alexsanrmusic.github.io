I’m sitting by the window pane
Just thinking to myself
I’ve got a thirst that’s driving me insane
But there’s no silver on the shelf

You see them folks, my darlin’ girl
They drink that whiskey just like rain
But you won’t spare a single dime
You’d rather spend it on a gold-link chain

And now I’m stumbling home so blind
I’ve had a drop too much to drink
My poor old head is all beat up
With two holes where I used to think

Open up the door, my sweet
Don’t leave me sleeping in the yard
Just lay me down and hold me close
I’ll love you ‘til my heart stops beating hard

Please, sugar, open up the door
I’ll love you forevermore...
